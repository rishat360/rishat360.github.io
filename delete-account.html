Delete your Cashora account · অ্যাকাউন্ট মুছুন

From the app (instant)

  1. Open Cashora and go to More → Account (আরও → অ্যাকাউন্ট).
  2. Tap Delete account (অ্যাকাউন্ট মুছুন). You can first save all transactions as a CSV file.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account, all synced records (transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, notes, people, reminders), your device list and sign-in history are deleted from our servers immediately and cannot be recovered.

Without the app

Email sayful7228@gmail.com from the email address (or with the phone number) you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Cashora account". We delete the account and all its data within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is kept

Nothing is kept after deletion. Receipt photos and any data stored only on your phone are removed when you uninstall the app.
