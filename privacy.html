Privacy Policy · গোপনীয়তা নীতি

Last updated: 29 September 2026

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google we receive only your name, email address and profile picture (scopes: openid, email, profile). With email/phone sign-in we store your name, email or phone number. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and stored only as a secure hash.
  • Google Drive backup (optional): if you turn on "Google Drive auto backup", Cashora asks for the drive.file permission and saves copies of your records as files in a "Cashora Backup" folder in your own Google Drive. This permission only lets Cashora see and change the files it created itself; it cannot see any other file in your Drive. You can turn it off in the app or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Your records: the transactions, accounts, categories, notes and other entries you create are stored on your phone and, when signed in, in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your account so they sync between your devices.
  • Devices and sign-ins: device name, Android version, app version and sign-in times, so you can see and sign out your devices. We do not store IP addresses or location.
  • Receipt photos you attach are stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. They are never uploaded. The camera is opened only when you tap "Take photo"; Cashora does not request camera or storage permissions.
  • Budgets, savings goals, payment methods and tags you create are part of your records and sync the same way.
  • Notifications (reminders, budget alerts, new sign-in alerts) are created on your phone.
  • Cashora has no ads, no analytics and no tracking SDKs.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored encrypted at rest by Google Cloud. Backup files you export are encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256). You can lock the app with your phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock.

How we use it

Only to run the app: signing you in, syncing and backing up your own records. We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or use it for tracking or advertising.

Who can see it

Only you, when signed in to your account. Data is protected by Firebase security rules so each account can read and write only its own data. Google (Firebase) processes the data as our service provider.

Google user data

Cashora's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The Google data we receive (name, email, profile picture) is used only to identify your account. Google Drive access (drive.file, optional) is used only to save and restore your own backup files at your request; the files stay in your Drive and are never read by us or shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

In the app go to More → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all your cloud data immediately. You can also request deletion without the app. Data on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
