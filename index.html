Cashora

Cashora is a personal finance app for Android. The purpose of Cashora is to help people record their daily income and expenses, track money they owe or are owed, plan budgets and shopping lists, and see simple reports of where their money goes.

Cashora একটি ব্যক্তিগত হিসাব রাখার অ্যান্ড্রয়েড অ্যাপ: আয়-ব্যয়, পাওনা-দেনা, বাজেট ও বাজারের তালিকা এক জায়গায়।

What Cashora does

  • Record income, expenses and transfers between cash, bank and mobile wallets
  • Track money lent and borrowed, with reminders
  • Reports, charts and a monthly calendar
  • Works fully offline; available in English and Bangla
  • Optional sign-in to sync the same records across your phones and keep them safe if a phone is lost
  • Monthly budgets with alerts, and savings goals
  • Receipt photos, payment methods, personal / business tags
  • Smart spending analysis, net worth and PDF reports
  • App lock using the phone's fingerprint or screen lock

How Cashora uses Google Sign-In

You can sign in to Cashora with your Google account. Cashora receives only your name, email address and profile picture, and uses them only to create and identify your Cashora account so your records sync between your devices. Cashora never sees your Google password and does not access your Gmail, Drive or contacts.
